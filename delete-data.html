Delete specific data

Last updated: August 29, 2026

You don't need to delete your entire SnowKeep account to remove specific pieces of your data. This page covers requesting deletion of individual items while keeping your account active. To delete your whole account instead, see the account deletion page.

What you can delete directly, in the app

Most data in SnowKeep can already be deleted yourself, without contacting anyone:

Tasks

Delete any task from the Tasks module directly — it's removed immediately.

Medications & logs

Delete a medication or an individual dose log entry directly from the Medications module.

Linked bank accounts

Unlink a Plaid-connected bank account from the Financial module — this revokes SnowKeep's access and removes the associated balance and transaction data.

What requires a request to the developer

Some data isn't yet exposed through a self-service delete control in the app. For these, request removal directly:

  1. Email this address from your account's associated email address, with the subject line "Delete specific data — SnowKeep".
  2. Describe exactly which data you want removed (for example, historical financial transactions from an already-unlinked account, email account data, or specific document references).
  3. The developer will confirm and complete the request, typically within a few days, without deleting your account or any other data.

Questions about this process? Contact this address.
