Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

SnowKeep is a private, self-hosted application built for one household's personal use. It is not a commercial product, has no public sign-up, and is not distributed to the general public. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and how it's protected — written to the same standard as a public app, even though SnowKeep isn't one.

Who this applies to

SnowKeep is used exclusively by its developer (Raii Winters) and one caregiver, who has account access scoped to a subset of the app's features. There is no public registration, no other users, and no plan to expand access beyond these two individuals.

What data SnowKeep collects and why

Category What's collected Why
Account info Username, email, password (hashed) To sign in to the app
Financial data Bank account balances, transaction history (via Plaid) Personal budgeting and financial tracking
Health data Medication names, dosages, schedules, and dose logs (taken/skipped) Medication reminders and adherence tracking
Personal documents Files and notes stored via the connected Nextcloud account Household and family document management
Task data To-do items, due dates, assignments Household and personal task tracking
Email Email messages and account credentials for a connected mail account To provide an email client within the app
Home automation Light/switch states via the connected Home Assistant instance Smart home control from within the app

Financial data, medication data, and task data are all stored on the developer's own self-hosted infrastructure (PocketBase), not on any third-party cloud platform. Documents live on the developer's own self-hosted Nextcloud instance. Home automation data stays on the developer's local network and is never transmitted to any third party.

Third-party services

Plaid

SnowKeep uses Plaid to securely connect to bank accounts and retrieve balance and transaction data. Bank login credentials are never seen, transmitted, or stored by SnowKeep — authentication happens entirely within Plaid's own secure Link flow. SnowKeep does not initiate payments, transfers, or any financial transactions; it is read-only. See Plaid's own privacy policy for how Plaid itself handles data in transit.

No other third party has access to SnowKeep's data. Data is never sold, rented, or shared for advertising or marketing purposes of any kind — SnowKeep does not use advertising, analytics trackers, or any advertising identifier.

How data is protected

  • All network traffic between the app and its backend is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS.
  • All data is stored on infrastructure owned and operated by the developer — no third-party cloud database or storage provider is used for app data.
  • Bank credentials are never stored by SnowKeep at any point — only Plaid ever sees them.
  • Account access is scoped per user — the caregiver's account can only see and act on the specific modules and data explicitly granted to it.
  • Sensitive data is end-to-end encrypted at rest using per-user encryption keys generated and stored only on-device; the server never has access to the decryption keys or plaintext. An optional on-device App Lock (PIN/biometric) can further restrict access to the app itself.

Data retention and deletion

Data is retained for as long as an account remains active. Either user may request deletion of their account and all associated data at any time by contacting the developer directly. Because this is a closed, two-user application, deletion requests can typically be fulfilled immediately.

Children's privacy

SnowKeep is not directed at children and is not used by anyone under the age of 18.

Changes to this policy

If what SnowKeep collects or how it's handled changes meaningfully, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect that change.
