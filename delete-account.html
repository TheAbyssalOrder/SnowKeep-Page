Delete your account

Last updated: August 29, 2026

SnowKeep is a private, closed application developed and operated by Raii Winters, used only by two named individuals. There is no self-service account deletion inside the app itself, since account management is handled directly by the developer for both users. This page explains how to request deletion.

How to request deletion

  1. Email this address from the email address associated with your SnowKeep account, with the subject line "Delete my SnowKeep account".
  2. State clearly whether you want your entire account deleted, or only specific data (for example, medication history, or a linked bank connection) removed while keeping your account active.
  3. The developer will confirm your identity directly, since this is a closed, personally-known-user application, and complete the request.

Because SnowKeep has only two users, requests are typically completed within a few days of being received.

What gets deleted

Data type On full account deletion
Account & login credentials Permanently deleted
Financial data (via Plaid) Deleted; the Plaid connection is also unlinked, which revokes SnowKeep's access to your bank data
Medication data & logs Permanently deleted
Task data Permanently deleted
Email account data Permanently deleted
Nextcloud & Home Assistant connection references Permanently deleted (the underlying Nextcloud/Home Assistant accounts themselves, if any, are managed separately and are not affected)

Deleted data is removed from SnowKeep's active database. Standard infrastructure backups may retain deleted data for a limited period afterward before being cycled out, but are not accessible through the app and are not used for any purpose other than disaster recovery.
